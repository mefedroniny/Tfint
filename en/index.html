About TF International

We are a dynamically growing and flexible Czech company focused on crisis management — the so-called Task Force — in Operations, as well as on technical support and development of internal and corporate processes or projects in their various phases.

We also provide our clients with services in innovation, design and development of chemical products under the client's private label, or act as a partner for external research and development.

Our team has been active on the European and international stage for several years. As a result, our people offer long-standing experience and expertise in:
